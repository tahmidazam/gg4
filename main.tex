  \section*{System identification}

  $n_x = 6$ was read from the singular-value spectrum of the block Hankel matrix
  (\autoref{fig:latent_dim}): six singular values exceed the relative threshold
  $10^{-6}\sigma_1$, with the remainder at numerical zero. ERA and CVA+EM were
  both fitted with $n_x = 6$. CVA+EM showed higher mean absolute error in both
  magnitude and phase across all $n_u n_y = 32$ input--output channels
  (\autoref{fig:bode}), and its transfer function failed to reproduce the
  per-neuron frequency selectivity for input $u_1$ that ERA recovered
  (\autoref{fig:freq_map}). One-step-ahead Kalman prediction on $N = 50$
  held-out trials ($T = 2000$ steps) reversed this ordering: CVA+EM achieved a
  higher median Frobenius $R^2_F$ ($0.399$ vs $0.289$;
  \autoref{fig:obs_reconstruction}). ERA was adopted on the basis of its
  frequency-domain accuracy.
