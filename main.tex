  \section*{System identification}

  $n_x = 6$ was read from the singular-value spectrum of the block Hankel matrix
  (\autoref{fig:latent_dim}): six singular values exceed the relative threshold
  $10^{-6}\sigma_1$, with the remainder at numerical zero. All models were
  fitted with $n_x = 6$.

  CVA+EM was fitted first. Its noise matrices, estimated jointly with the
  dynamics on time-series data, yielded $\operatorname{tr}(Q)/\operatorname{tr}(R)
    = 0.077$, and one-step-ahead Kalman prediction on $N = 50$ held-out trials
  ($T = 2000$ steps) gave median $R^2_F = 0.399$ ($84\%$ of the noise-floor
  ceiling; \autoref{fig:obs_reconstruction}). The downstream muscle decoder maps
  neural activity to four muscle activation rates through band-pass filters tuned
  to specific frequencies in the decoded latent signal, so a model that
  misrepresents the frequency response will produce systematically incorrect
  muscle activations regardless of state-reconstruction quality. CVA+EM's mean
  magnitude MAE was $0.086$ across all $n_u n_y = 32$ channels, concentrated
  at low frequencies; phase MAE was $49^\circ$, distributed approximately
  uniformly across frequency (\autoref{fig:bode}). Its transfer function
  reproduced the per-neuron selectivity for $u_1$ but failed to recover that
  for $u_2$ (\autoref{fig:freq_map}).

  ERA reduced the magnitude MAE to $0.017$ and phase MAE to $5^\circ$,
  recovering the empirical per-neuron selectivity for both $u_1$ and $u_2$.
  Its eigenvalue spectrum
  contains one complex conjugate pair at $\pm 0.0274$ cycles per step
  ($|z| = 0.951$); CVA+EM's nearest pair lies at $\pm 0.0026$ cycles per step,
  an order of magnitude lower. One-step-ahead prediction degraded to $R^2_F =
    0.289$ ($51\%$ of ceiling). The Yule-Walker noise estimate produced
  $\operatorname{tr}(Q) = 1666$ against $\operatorname{tr}(R) = 11.4$, a ratio
  of $146$, so the Kalman filter received negligible information from its own
  state prediction (\autoref{fig:id}).

  Applying EM to ERA-initialised matrices (ERA+EM) corrected the noise
  calibration without disturbing the eigenvalue structure. The process noise
  fell to $\operatorname{tr}(Q) = 0.674$ (ratio $0.048$); the dominant complex
  pair shifted from $0.0274$ to $0.0246$ cycles per step. Observation
  reconstruction recovered to $R^2_F = 0.399$, matching CVA+EM
  (\autoref{fig:obs_reconstruction}); the ordering ERA $<$ CVA+EM $\approx$
  ERA+EM was consistent across all individual output channels. Frequency
  accuracy (magnitude MAE $0.049$, phase $23^\circ$) remained substantially
  better than CVA+EM (\autoref{fig:bode}). ERA+EM was adopted.
