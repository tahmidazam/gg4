Spectral tracking metrics for LQG, LQI, and MPC controllers using the ERA+EM $n_x=4$ model ($n_x = 4$). Each controller was tuned by random search (8 trials, seed 1) and evaluated over $N = 20$ independent brain seeds. The target was a sum of sinusoids at $f \in \{0.01, 0.03\}$ cycles per sample with amplitudes $A \in \{1.50, 1.50\}$, centred on a physical mean of 4. Bars show the mean; error bars show the standard error of the mean. Left: spectral RMS error (RMS amplitude deviation at target frequencies). Centre: maximum amplitude error (worst-case per-component deviation).
