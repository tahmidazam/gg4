Per-metric comparison of the default and Bayesian-optimised Phase-B controllers on the full 24-target grid (mean\,$\pm$\,SD across targets). TTT is the time to threshold in simulation steps; sector fraction measures the proportion of the trajectory spent in the sector pointing toward the target; rotational distance is the total angular sweep of the hand about the target. The optimiser leaves final distance, reach rate, and TTT unchanged while reducing rotational distance and path length and raising sector fraction (\autoref{fig:cartesian_control_sweep}). \href{https://github.com/tahmidazam/gg4/blob/main/notebooks/cartesian_control_sweep/cartesian_control_sweep.ipynb}{\underline{Source}}%
