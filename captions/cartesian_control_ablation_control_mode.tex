Ablation comparing open-loop (sinusoidal drive) and closed-loop LQI control modes on 24 targets across $N=10$ Brain seeds ($T=1000$ steps). Bars show mean across seeds; error bars and dots show ±\,1\,SD and individual seed means, respectively. Four metrics are shown: final distance to target, sector fraction, time-to-threshold, and control effort.
