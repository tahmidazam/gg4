Neural correlates of muscle selection. For each of the four selection frequencies identified in \autoref{fig:muscle_selection_freq_sweep}, a sustained open-loop sinusoidal trial ($u_0 = 0.5 + 0.4\sin(2\pi f^* t)$, $u_1 = 0.5$) recorded $n_y = 16$\,D neural observations over 1024 steps following 500 burn-in steps. Each line shows the amplitude at $\omega^*_\mathrm{sel}$ per neuron, normalised by the muscle\textquotesingle s own neuron-mean; the dashed line marks the mean ($= 1$). Profiles that collapse onto each other confirm that every muscle recruits the same relative neural activation pattern, so the $16$\,D\,$\to$\,$x_1$ projection is not muscle-specific. \href{https://github.com/tahmidazam/gg4/blob/main/notebooks/neural_correlates/neural_correlates.ipynb}{\underline{Source}}%
